About Me

Systems Engineer by Mindset

Senior Mobile Engineer with 6+ years building production-grade platforms across fintech, B2B distribution, ERP, POS, restaurant management, and enterprise SaaS — contributing to apps that reach 10M+ users across iOS & Android.

My path started unconventionally: B.E. Mechanical → Data Science (Python, ML, TensorFlow) → self-taught Flutter engineer, driven by a real problem I saw in my family's FMCG distribution business. I don't just build features — I build complete systems: architecture, security, CI/CD, observability, release engineering, and long-term maintainability. Fintech-grade quality is my default.
